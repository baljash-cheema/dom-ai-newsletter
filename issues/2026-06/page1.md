## Welcome

Welcome to the inaugural issue of the **DOM Education Newsletter** — a monthly
briefing that connects educators, program leaders, coordinators, and trainees
across the Department of Medicine. Each month we take a closer look at one topic
shaping education across the DOM, alongside the events, policies, and resources
you need to know. **Turn to page 2** for this month's *AI in Medical Education* feature.
